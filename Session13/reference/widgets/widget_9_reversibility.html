What Reversibility Frees

A reversible network rebuilds the activations of each layer during the backward pass, so it keeps almost none of them. This is one dense model of 30.2 billion parameters on one node of eight B200 GPUs with 167.6 GiB free on each card, at tensor parallelism 8 and sequence parallelism 8.
