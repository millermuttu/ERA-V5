Freezing Parts of the Model

Our model has 78.1 billion parameters. A trainable weight costs 16 bytes (2 weight + 2 gradient + 4 master copy + 8 Adam state), and a frozen weight costs 2 bytes. Freeze one or more parts to see the memory saving and the constraints that each frozen part adds.
