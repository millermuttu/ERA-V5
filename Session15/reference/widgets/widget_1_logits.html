Attention Logits Without a Limit

An attention logit is the score q·k / √d that goes into the softmax. Without a fix, the largest logit can grow without a limit during training. Select a fix to see its effect on the largest logit and on the loss over 10,000 steps.

the four modes
  • none: logit = q·k / √d
  • QK-norm: normalize q and k before q·k
  • QK-Clip: scale = min(1, 100 / max logit)
  • soft-cap: logit = 50 × tanh(x / 50)

Fact: The team that trained our model removed QK-norm in a small test model. The test model diverged.
