From Logits to Expert Weights

The router gives one token a logit for each of the 8 experts, turns the logits into scores, keeps the top k scores, and rescales them into weights that add up to 1.

Softmax: the 8 scores compete with each other and always add up to 1.

Sigmoid: each score is computed from its own logit alone and lies between 0 and 1.

weight of each kept expert = its score / sum of the kept scores
