S1-1 · Activations exist for a reason

Two concentric rings. Two models. The only difference is one ReLU hidden layer.

A model with no nonlinearity computes σ(w₁x + w₂y + b), and its decision boundary is always the straight line w₁x + w₂y + b = 0. A ring surrounded by another ring can't be cut off by a straight line, so it's stuck near chance. Add one ReLU hidden layer and each unit contributes a clipped half-plane; their sum folds into a closed region that wraps the inner ring. Same data, same optimizer, same steps — only the activation changed.
