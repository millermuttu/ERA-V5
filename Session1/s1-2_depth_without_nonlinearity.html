S1-2 · Depth without nonlinearity is a lie

Same rings. Three models. Five linear layers are exactly one linear layer — until ReLU breaks the tie.

Stack five linear layers and you get W₅(W₄(W₃(W₂(W₁x)))), and matrix products collapse: that whole tower is one matrix W₅W₄W₃W₂W₁ times x, plus one bias. So a 5-layer linear net can only draw the same straight line a 1-layer net draws — the extra layers are decoration. Insert ReLU between the same five layers and the product can no longer be multiplied out; the network finally buys something with its depth.
