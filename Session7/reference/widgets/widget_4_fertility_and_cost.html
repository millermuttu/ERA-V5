Fertility and Embedding Cost

The tokenizer decides how much attention compute each language costs. The embedding system decides how much parameter memory that vocabulary costs. They pull in opposite directions, they are set by the same number, and for an India-first model the optimum sits somewhere a monolingual model would never choose.

Read the fertility numbers as assumptions, not measurements. The starting values are estimates, and the curve moving them with vocabulary size is a model: fertility decays toward an asymptote of one token per word, so each language's reducible headroom is its starting fertility minus one. English begins at 1.30 and has only 0.30 to gain; Tamil begins at 3.70 and has 2.70. That asymmetry is what makes the corpus mix move the optimum, so it is the assumption to attack first. Replace all of it with the fertilities you measured in the Session 2 tokenizer assignment before you use this to defend a number.

Fertility sensitivity
How much a bigger vocabulary actually helps. It is the exponent in the decay: every doubling of the vocabulary removes of whatever fertility headroom a language has left. Set it to zero and extra rows buy nothing. This is an empirical property of your tokenizer, and it is precisely what the Session 2 assignment measured, so replace it with your own number.
Price of memory
The exchange rate between the two bills. Doubling the vocabulary from the 32,768 reference adds to the combined index, so the token bill has to fall by more than that for the extra rows to be worth buying. This is a judgement, not a measurement: it depends on your hardware and on whether you are memory-bound or compute-bound. It moves where the optimum sits, but not which way it travels when the corpus mix changes.

The two bills against vocabulary size

Both bills are expressed against the same reference vocabulary of 32,768, where each equals 1.0, so they share an axis honestly. The token bill is the mean fertility of your mix, which is what every layer of attention pays on every sequence for the whole of training and the whole of inference; it falls as the vocabulary grows. The parameter bill is the two token-facing matrices; it rises in a straight line. The combined index adds them at the memory price you set, and its minimum is marked. Change the corpus mix and the minimum moves, because a mix with more reducible fertility has more to gain from extra rows.
