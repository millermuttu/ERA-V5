Frozen Input Path Lab

Two models are trained in your browser from the identical initialisation on the identical data stream, and until the transition they are the same run. At the moment the data mixture shifts, one of them freezes its embedding table and the other does not. Real forward passes, real softmax cross-entropy, hand-written gradient descent: every number below was computed by arithmetic this page performed, so the whole difference between the two curves after the marker is the one decision.
