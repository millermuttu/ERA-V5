Weight Tying Board

Two matrices of identical shape face the same vocabulary, one reading tokens and one predicting them. Tying uses a single matrix for both jobs and removes an entire billion-parameter tensor. The question is not whether the saving is real, it is whether the constraint costs more than the saving buys, and the answer moves with scale.
