Data Parallelism

Four GPUs hold one copy each of the same four weights, and the batch of eight samples is split two per GPU. The loss on a sample is L = 0.5 · ||w − x||2, so that sample’s gradient is w − x, and a GPU’s gradient is the mean over its two samples. The update is w := w − 0.1 g.
