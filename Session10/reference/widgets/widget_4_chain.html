← the gradient travels right to left. Each link multiplies it by one more local number, and that is the whole of the chain rule.

Each link, with its multiplication

1

dL/dy

The loss is the gap squared, so its slope at the output is twice the gap.

2

dL/dw2

y is w2 times h. Raise w2 by one and y moves by h, so multiply by h.

3

dL/dh

h feeds the same y through w2. Raise h by one and y moves by w2.

4

dL/dw1

h is w1 times x. One more multiplication and the first weight has its gradient.
